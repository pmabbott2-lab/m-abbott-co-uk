About MortgageEasy

Advisor-led mortgage advice, supported by modern technology.

MortgageEasy is a UK mortgage brokerage built around qualified human advice. We use digital tools — calculator illustrations, voice and chat fact-finds, and online booking — so customers arrive prepared and advisors can focus on what matters: the right recommendation for you.

Who we are

MortgageEasy was founded on a simple belief: mortgage advice should feel personal, clear, and efficient. Too many people repeat the same details to several firms, or arrive at their first advisor meeting without the basics organised. We built a customer journey that respects your time while keeping a qualified advisor at the centre of every recommendation.
