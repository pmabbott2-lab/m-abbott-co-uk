How it works

What the calculator does — and doesn’t.

The estimate

The calculator works out a monthly repayment for a capital-and-interest mortgage from the property value, your deposit (or outstanding balance when remortgaging) and the term you choose. The rate is an illustrative average for your loan-to-value band.

Key assumptions

  • Interest is compounded monthly.
  • The illustrative rate applies for the full term.
  • Fees, product incentives and lender criteria are not included.
  • It does not replace a full affordability assessment.
Important — illustrative figures only

This calculator gives an estimate based on the figures you enter. Rates shown are not mortgage offers, quotations or recommendations. Your home may be repossessed if you do not keep up repayments on your mortgage. See our disclosures for more information.
