About Peter

Peter Mabbott

Founder & Managing Director

Peter brings over 25 years’ financial services experience spanning retail banking, mortgage distribution, intermediary services and executive leadership.

Having held senior leadership positions within one of the UK’s largest banking groups and one of the UK’s largest mortgage distribution businesses, he has built deep expertise in commercial leadership, business transformation, adviser development and operational excellence.

Trent Valley Financial Services brings that experience home. It is a return to measured, relationship-led advice for families and professional colleagues who want clarity without the noise.
